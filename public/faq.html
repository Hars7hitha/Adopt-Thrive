Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pet Adoption and Rescue?

We are an organization dedicated to finding loving homes for abandoned and stray pets. Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need.

How can I adopt a pet?

Browse our available pets, contact us or the rescue center, and follow the adoption process detailed on our website.

How can I submit a pet for rescue?

Visit our "Submit a Pet" page, fill out the form with the necessary details, and upload a photo of the pet in need.

How can I make a donation?

Visit our "Donate" page where you will find the options and instructions for making a donation to support our cause.

How can I volunteer?

Contact us through our "Contact Us" page to learn about volunteer opportunities and how you can get involved.
